GoPiano

A playable multi-touch piano for iPhone and iPad.

Hold down chords, slide between keys, record what you play, then watch it back on the keyboard or share it as a video with the keys lighting up.

Support

Found a bug, or something not working as you expect? Open an issue and it will be read.

Report a problem or ask a question
No account details needed beyond a free GitHub account.

When reporting a bug, it helps to say which device and iOS version you are on, and what you did just before it happened.

Privacy

GoPiano collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking, and it never asks for microphone access. See the privacy policy for the full statement.

Source

GoPiano is open source. The code is on GitHub.
