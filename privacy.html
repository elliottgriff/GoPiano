Privacy Policy

GoPiano for iPhone and iPad

Last updated 11 October 2026

GoPiano does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server: the app has no way to send anything anywhere.

What the app stores

Melodies you record are saved on your device, in the app's own folder. You can see them in the Files app under GoPiano, and delete them there or in the app at any time. Deleting GoPiano removes them with it.

The app also remembers small preferences on your device — how many octaves to show, whether note names are displayed, and the reverb level. These never leave the device.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No tracking, and no data shared with third parties
  • No microphone access — GoPiano records its own sound from inside the app, so it never listens to the room, and iOS will never prompt you for microphone permission on its behalf
  • No contacts, photos, location, or health data
  • No network requests of any kind

Sharing a melody

When you share a melody or export it as a video, iOS hands the file to whichever app or person you choose. That is the only way anything you record leaves your device, and it happens only when you ask for it. Once shared, the file is subject to the privacy policy of wherever you sent it.

Children

GoPiano is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or anything else about the app, can be raised at the GoPiano issue tracker.
