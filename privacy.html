Privacy Policy

GoPiano for iPhone and iPad

Last updated 27 November 2026

GoPiano does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server: the app has no way to send anything anywhere.

What the app stores

Melodies you record, and voice memos or MIDI files you import, are saved on your device, in the app's own folder, together with the notes that were played or sung in them. You can see them in the Files app under GoPiano, and delete them there or in the app at any time. Deleting GoPiano removes them with it.

The app also remembers small preferences on your device — the instrument, how many octaves to show, note names and colours, and metronome settings. These never leave the device.

The microphone

GoPiano uses the microphone only when you choose Voice, so it can show the notes you sing, hum or whistle. iOS asks your permission the first time, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

  • The microphone is on only while Voice is chosen. Choosing an instrument turns it off, and iOS shows its microphone indicator whenever it is on.
  • The pitch of your voice is worked out on your device. No audio is sent anywhere.
  • Your voice is kept only if you record a take while singing. The take is saved on your device like any other melody, and you can delete it.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No tracking, and no data shared with third parties
  • No listening in the background, and no audio sent anywhere — see the microphone above
  • No contacts, photos, location, or health data
  • No network requests of any kind

Sharing a melody

When you share a melody, or export it as a video, MIDI file or sheet music, iOS hands the file to whichever app or person you choose. That is the only way anything you record leaves your device, and it happens only when you ask for it. Once shared, the file is subject to the privacy policy of wherever you sent it.

Children

GoPiano is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or anything else about the app, can be raised at the GoPiano issue tracker.
